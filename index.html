Keep them fresh

01

Clean often

Brush off dirt, then wipe with a damp cloth and mild soap. Skip the washing machine.

02

Let them dry naturally away from direct sun or heaters, which can warp glue and soles.

03

Give each pair a day off so the cushioning bounces back and moisture clears out.

04

Keep them in a cool, dry place and stuff with paper to hold their shape.
